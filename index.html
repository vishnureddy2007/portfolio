CrimeVision – AI Crime Investigation System

An offline-capable forensic analysis and investigation workspace engineered for law enforcement evidence analysis and timeline reconstruction.

Forensic AI Evidence Analyzer & Video Generator

Python 3.10+ • Streamlit • YOLOv8 Multi-Model • OpenCV • Pillow • Qwen3 14B • 25/25 Passing Tests

GitHub Repository

The Crime Investigation AI pipeline operates entirely offline on secure hardware without sending sensitive investigative data to external third-party cloud servers:

Stage 01
Media Intake & Preprocessing
Upload crime scene photos & CCTV video streams. Keyframe extraction, EXIF metadata extraction, and timestamp normalization.
Stage 02
Multi-Source Detection
YOLOv8 multi-model pipeline (general COCO + dedicated weapon model). Cross-model IoU / IoS bounding box deduplication.
Stage 03
4-Stage Weapon Verifier
False positive shield assessing aspect ratios, scale, model confidence, and temporal persistence. Strict candidate isolation.
Stage 04
Risk & Narrative Modeling
Evidence severity scoring coupled with local LLM (Qwen3 14B) generating cautious, structured forensic investigative narratives.
Stage 05
2D Video & PDF Export
Pure Python video generator with dynamic Ken Burns zoom onto evidence, plus comprehensive PDF & DOCX audit logs.

4-Stage Weapon Verification

Eliminates false alarms from shadows or innocuous handheld objects. Validates firearm subtypes (Revolver, Pistol, Rifle, Shotgun, Knife) via scale, aspect ratio, and multi-frame temporal persistence.

Python-Native 2D Video Rendering

Renders high-definition 1080p forensic investigation videos using pure OpenCV and Pillow without external 3D software or Blender dependencies. Features smooth Ken Burns zoom onto evidence.

Cautious Forensic Narrative Generation

Utilizes local Qwen3 14B model (via Ollama) or deterministic local fallback to produce structured, objective summaries adhering to strict legal and forensic evidentiary standards.

Responsible AI and Evidentiary Notice

Crime Investigation AI is strictly designed as an investigative decision-support workspace. System detections, weapon classifications, and AI-generated narratives do not establish legal guilt, constitute infallible proof of weapon possession, or replace certified human forensic examination. Unverified weapon candidates are strictly segregated from formal threat scoring to protect civil liberties and prevent investigative bias.
